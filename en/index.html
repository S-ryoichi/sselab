IoT Security / True Random Numbers / FPGA

Welcome to Secure System Engineering Laboratory.

In this laboratory, we are conducting research on IoT security, with cryptography circuit implementation being our primary focus area, particularly concerning the noise source of the entropy source for encryption circuits. Additionally, we are engaged in research related to building IoT systems for digitizing and analyzing various physical phenomena, with the goal of promoting digital transformation in industry. When developing IoT system prototypes, we place particular emphasis on implementing security measures to ensure secure data transmission and reception.
